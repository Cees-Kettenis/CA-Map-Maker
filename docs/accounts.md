# Account setup

Public signup is closed. Ask the administrator to create your account. You will receive an email with a single-use link to choose a password of at least 12 characters. The link expires after one hour; use **Forgot your password?** to request another if needed.

## First installation

On a fresh database, the site opens a setup wizard. Enter your admin email and follow the email link to choose your password. The app stays closed until you finish, then signs you in and opens Settings for the shared Campfire token. The wizard closes afterward. Existing accounts and passwords skip this setup when their database is restored.

## Administrator

Open **Accounts**, enter the new user's email, and select **Create user**. They receive a password setup email. No password is displayed or sent. If delivery fails, the account is not created and you can retry. Only the administrator can list accounts, create users, or manage the shared Campfire token.

## Signing in

On the login page, choose **Log in with email** to receive a sign-in link, or
enter your password. The password buttons let you choose whether to stay
logged in on that device.

## Account settings

Open **Settings** to change your email or password. Email changes require
confirmation at the new address. Sensitive changes may ask you to log in again.

Imports use the administrator's [shared Campfire token](campfire-credentials.md). Regular users do not provide a token.

## Password recovery

Choose **Forgot your password?** on the login page and enter your email.
Follow the recovery link and enter matching passwords of at least 12 characters.

Recovery links expire after one hour and work once. Resetting your password
signs out existing sessions.

## Delete your account

Open **Settings**, type your current email in **Delete your account**, and confirm deletion. You may need to sign in again first. This removes your owned maps, meetups, communities, invitations, imports, sessions, jobs and exclusive image files. Other accounts keep images they still use. Backups and copies already downloaded by browsers are outside the live app's deletion scope.
